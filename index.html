About Me

EB

I am an Associate Professor at the LBJ School of Public Affairs at The University of Texas at Austin. I received my Ph.D. in Political Science from the University of Oklahoma in 2019.

My research looks for ways to cut the needless hassles that keep people from public services, make government programs work better, and rebuild trust in government. It has two aims: diagnosing where policy design and implementation break down, and designing and testing interventions that fix them. Much of this work focuses on college access and affordability, including tuition-free college and Promise programs.

Before joining the LBJ School, I taught at Miami University and Florida State University. From 2020 to 2024 I was an Associate Fellow and Academic Affiliate with the Office of Evaluation Sciences at the U.S. General Services Administration, where I ran randomized controlled trials to reduce burdens in federal programs. I have partnered with the Department of Education, the Small Business Administration, the IRS, the Social Security Administration, the Oklahoma State Regents for Higher Education, and Tulsa Community College.

My work appears in the Journal of Public Administration Research and Theory, Public Administration Review, the Journal of Politics, the Journal of Policy Analysis and Management, and the American Educational Research Journal, among others. Current projects, funded by Arnold Ventures and the Greater Texas Foundation, evaluate the return on investment of free college programs in Texas.
